Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Gwahakppyong Uploader ("the App") handles data. The App is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Gwahakppyong channel.

1. Data we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit, the App receives the basic profile of the owner's own TikTok account (open ID, display name and avatar) and an access token, only for the permissions the owner approves (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

2. How we use it

The data is used only to show which account is signed in and to upload the channel's own videos to that account. We do not access, collect or process data of any other TikTok user.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never sent to any server other than TikTok's. We do not run a server or database for the App.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. We do not use the data for advertising.

5. Deleting data

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps and websites). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

6. Contact

Please contact us through a direct message to @gwahakppyong on TikTok or Instagram.
